The Solution

Our Solution: The Dual-Flywheel

We help solve the data problem with a symbiotic model built on Properties and People.

The Property Flywheel

We achieve breadth by onboarding millions of properties into our Property Ledger.

The foundational skeleton is a comprehensive database covering all ~28 million UK residential addresses.

Created by aggregating public and licensed datasets (Ordnance Survey, Land Registry, EPC Register, Verisk), it provides a baseline profile for every property from day one.

The People Flywheel

We achieve depth by giving homeowners a free HomeTruth AI advisor that enriches records with consented, ground-truth data.

The advisor provides accurate and trustworthy information, proactive maintenance guidance, secure digital vault capabilities, and impartial insight.

As homeowners engage, they co-create the deeper truth about their homes.

Trust By Design

Data, Privacy & Trust by Design

Architected for GDPR

Separate Property Ledger (property facts) from Identity Layer (personal data). Minimal personal data with clear homeowner consent and control.

Clear Legal Position

Legitimate interests for improving the Property Ledger. Contract or consent for advisor experience and marketing. Partners remain regulated decision-makers.

Simple human privacy policy with self-service access and deletion. Designed to address GDPR and liability questions from the ground up.
