# HomeTruth Investor Page Design Brief

## Objective
Present HomeTruth as a credible, investable B2B infrastructure company, not a consumer app. The page should communicate clarity, trust, and execution maturity within the first 30 seconds.

## Audience
- Early-stage and growth investors
- Strategic partners in property, insurance, and lending
- Advisors evaluating team quality and market timing

## Visual Direction
- **Theme:** Light, editorial, high-contrast
- **Tone:** Confident, precise, low-hype
- **Brand:** Retain HomeTruth palette (orange/cyan/purple/green) as accents, not as heavy fills
- **Typography:** Gill Sans family with improved hierarchy and readable line lengths

## UX Principles
- Make scanning effortless: strong section titles, concise subtitles, consistent card rhythm
- Keep critical metrics visible: market size, ARR targets, model phases, team credibility
- Reduce cognitive load: more whitespace, fewer dark surfaces, softer borders and shadows
- Preserve momentum: hero -> problem -> solution -> model -> team -> ask

## Content Principles
- Use short, declarative sentences
- Prefer evidence and outcomes over adjectives
- Ensure every section answers one investor question:
  - Why now?
  - Why this team?
  - Why this model?
  - Why it scales?

## Implementation Notes
- Keep single-file structure for speed (`index.html`)
- Use tokenized colors from existing variables
- Maintain gradient accents for brand continuity
- Avoid introducing new libraries or frameworks

## Success Criteria
- Readable on first pass without zooming or squinting
- Works well in a live investor call screen-share
- Feels premium and credible on desktop and mobile
- Preserves existing narrative but improves conversion confidence
