I Bought a House in Hastings. Here's What Nobody Told Me.

Personal founder story that makes the case for HomeTruth through lived experience. Warm, vulnerable, shareable.

MM
Monty Munford
Co-Founder & CEO at HomeTruth | Tech Journalist | Forbes, BBC, The Economist
Just now · 🌐

I've spent 20 years writing about technology.

I've covered AI, blockchain, fintech, and every flavour of "disruption" you can imagine. I've advised 40+ startups that have collectively raised over a billion dollars.

Then I bought a house in Hastings.

And I realised I knew absolutely nothing about the most important purchase of my life.

The survey flagged "some concerns" but didn't explain what they meant in plain English. The solicitor sent a tower of documents I didn't read because, honestly, who does? The insurance comparison site asked me questions I didn't know the answers to. I guessed.

Six months in, I discovered damp in a wall the surveyor didn't check. I found out my buildings insurance was based on market value, not rebuild cost — which meant I was significantly underinsured. And I had no idea when the boiler was last serviced, because the previous owner's records went in the skip.

I'm a tech journalist. I'm supposed to be good at this stuff. If I couldn't navigate it, what chance does everyone else have?

That's when it hit me.

My car has a logbook. Every service, every MOT, every owner — all recorded, all digital, all verified. I can check its history in 30 seconds on my phone.

My house — worth ten times more — had a cardboard box in the loft and a prayer.

So I called Jason Ryan, and we started building HomeTruth.

HomeTruth is the service log for your home. An AI advisor that actually helps you understand your property. A secure vault for every document. And eventually, the data layer that makes insurance, mortgages, and green finance work better for everyone.

We've graduated from the STYX accelerator. We've secured a $4M valuation. The MVP is live.

And we're just getting started.

If you've ever moved house and thought "this is insane" — you're right. It is. And we're fixing it.

Try it: hometruth.io — free, no signup, no agenda.

Jason Ryan · LinkedIn · Companion piece

When Monty called me and said "I just bought a house and the whole process is broken," I didn't need convincing.

I'd spent 15 years building digital platforms for brands like Nike, Porsche, and Barclays. I knew what good data infrastructure looked like. And I knew property didn't have it.

The UK housing stock is worth £9 trillion. The insurance market is £60 billion. And the data layer underneath both of them is essentially a filing cabinet from 1995.

So we started building the service log for homes.

Not just a document store. An AI-powered property companion that understands your home, flags risks, tracks maintenance, and gives you — the homeowner — actual control over your most valuable asset.

Monty brings the vision and the network. I bring the product and the data architecture. Together, we're building something the UK property market has never had: a single source of truth.
