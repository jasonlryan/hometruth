## 1. The Strategic Problem This Solves

HomeTruth has two audiences with fundamentally different needs — investors and homeowners — and the content strategy needs to serve both simultaneously.

The public website at [hometruth.io](https://hometruth.io) does a strong job of communicating the platform vision, the dual-flywheel model, and the market opportunity. It also hosts the live AI Advisor. But there is currently no ongoing content layer — no blog, no guides, no editorial, no SEO-driven articles — that gives homeowners a reason to discover HomeTruth organically, return regularly, and build trust before they engage with the product.

Meanwhile, the competitive landscape is moving fast. The UK government is actively exploring mandating digital property logbooks. Home Owner's Passport already has Land Registry integration and is piloting with estate agents. Hometree has 100,000+ customers for home services subscriptions. The window to establish HomeTruth as the definitive voice on property intelligence is open, but it won't stay open.

This content strategy adds the missing layer: it gives homeowners a reason to engage (feeding the consumer flywheel), it gives investors evidence of traction and market positioning, and it builds an organic acquisition engine that reduces CAC over time.

## 2. Audience Analysis

### 2.1 First-Time Buyers

**Who they are:** 25-38, likely renting, saving for a deposit, anxious and overwhelmed by the biggest financial commitment of their lives.

**What they feel:** Fear of making a mistake. Information overload. Distrust of estate agents. Imposter syndrome about "adult" financial decisions. Excitement mixed with dread.

**What they need from HomeTruth:**
- Guidance they can trust (not from someone trying to sell them something)
- Plain-English explainers of things everyone assumes they know (EPCs, surveys, conveyancing, insurance)
- Tools that make them feel more in control
- Someone on their side

**Where they are:** Google search, Instagram/TikTok, Reddit (r/UKPersonalFinance, r/HousingUK), first-time buyer forums, comparison sites

**Key search intent:** "what to check before buying a house", "first time buyer checklist UK", "do I need a full survey", "what is an EPC rating", "how much is home insurance for first time buyer"

### 2.2 Existing Homeowners

**Who they are:** 30-65, already own a home, juggling maintenance/insurance/improvements with busy lives. Ranges from first-time owners still learning to long-term homeowners who think they know it all.

**What they feel:** Vaguely anxious that they're not looking after their biggest asset properly. Frustrated by insurance opacity. Guilty about the boiler service they haven't booked. Curious about what their home is worth now.

**What they need from HomeTruth:**
- Practical help with the ongoing work of owning a home
- Reminders and schedules they don't have to think about
- Clarity on insurance (am I covered? am I overpaying?)
- Confidence that their home is maintaining or growing in value

**Where they are:** Google search, Facebook, local community groups, Mumsnet/parenting forums, home improvement content (YouTube, Pinterest)

**Key search intent:** "home maintenance schedule UK", "how often should I service my boiler", "is my home insurance enough", "how to increase property value", "what EPC rating do I need"

### 2.3 Landlords & Property Investors

**Who they are:** 35-65, own 1-10+ properties, focused on yield and compliance. Ranges from accidental landlords (inherited or couldn't sell) to professional portfolio managers.

**What they feel:** Stressed by ever-changing regulations. Overwhelmed by compliance requirements across multiple properties. Frustrated by tenant damage and maintenance costs. Worried about MEES and EPC obligations.

**What they need from HomeTruth:**
- Compliance tracking that actually works
- Multi-property document management
- Clarity on MEES regulations and EPC upgrade requirements
- Professional tools that save time and reduce risk

**Where they are:** Property investor forums, LinkedIn, landlord associations, specialist buy-to-let content, Google search

**Key search intent:** "landlord EPC requirements 2026", "MEES regulations UK", "property management software UK", "landlord compliance checklist", "how to manage rental property documents"

### 2.4 Investors & Partners (B2B)

**Who they are:** Seed-stage investors, insurtechs, insurance executives, mortgage brokers, green finance providers.

**What they feel:** Looking for data-backed opportunities. Sceptical of another PropTech pitch. Interested in the verified data angle and the regulatory tailwind.

**What they need from HomeTruth:**
- Evidence of consumer traction and product-market fit
- Market analysis that demonstrates the data opportunity
- Clear articulation of the business model and moat
- Proof that the team can execute

**Where they are:** LinkedIn, investor networks, PropTech events, industry press (Tech.eu, Sifted, PropertyWire)

## 3. The Creative Platform

### 3.1 The Underlying Human Truth

> "My home is the biggest investment of my life, but I know more about my car than I do about my house."

This is the tension HomeTruth exists to resolve. Cars have service histories, MOT records, and logbooks. Homes — worth ten times more — have a drawer full of paper and a vague memory of when the boiler was last serviced. The information asymmetry is absurd, and everyone feels it even if they can't articulate it.

### 3.2 The Creative Platform

**"Every home has a truth. Know yours."**

This is the idea that unites everything. It works across all audiences:

- **For homeowners:** Your home has a story — its real condition, its true value, its hidden risks, its untapped potential. HomeTruth helps you see it clearly.
- **For investors:** The UK property market runs on bad data. HomeTruth is building the infrastructure to fix that. The truth is the product.
- **For the industry:** Insurers, lenders, and brokers are making billion-pound decisions on incomplete information. HomeTruth gives them the truth they need.

The word "truth" is the brand's superpower. It implies transparency, trust, data integrity, and honesty — all the things the property market currently lacks. Every piece of content should make the reader feel like they're getting closer to the truth about their home.

### 3.3 Brand Voice Across Audiences

| Audience | Voice register | Think of it as... |
|---|---|---|
| First-time buyers | Warm, encouraging, jargon-free | A knowledgeable friend who's been through it |
| Existing homeowners | Practical, confident, slightly wry | The smart neighbour who just knows things |
| Landlords | Professional, efficient, regulation-savvy | A trusted compliance advisor |
| Investors | Data-grounded, visionary, commercially sharp | A founder who's done the homework |

## 4. Content Pillars

Four pillars. Each serves multiple audiences. Each has a clear editorial mission.

### Pillar 1: Know Your Home
**Editorial mission:** Help homeowners understand what they own — its value, its risks, its documents, its true condition.

**Why it matters:** This is the acquisition engine. Every homeowner who searches "what EPC rating do I need" or "how much is my house worth" is a potential user. This pillar turns HomeTruth into a trusted resource before anyone downloads the app.

| Audience | Content examples |
|---|---|
| First-time buyers | "The first-time buyer's document checklist", "What your survey actually tells you", "EPCs explained in 5 minutes" |
| Existing homeowners | "10 things you should know about your home but probably don't", "What's in your deeds (and why it matters)", "How to read your home insurance policy" |
| Landlords | "The landlord's compliance Bible", "Document management for multi-property portfolios" |

**Formats:** Long-form guides, interactive checklists, short explainer videos, infographics, AI advisor demos
**Primary channel:** Blog/SEO, YouTube, social media
**KPI:** Organic traffic, time on page, email signups

### Pillar 2: Protect Your Investment
**Editorial mission:** Demystify home insurance, reduce financial risk, and help homeowners make smarter money decisions about their property.

**Why it matters:** Insurance is HomeTruth's first B2B application. Content that exposes the data gap in insurance builds both consumer awareness and investor confidence. The stat that 70% of UK properties are underinsured is a content goldmine.

| Audience | Content examples |
|---|---|
| First-time buyers | "Home insurance: the complete guide for new homeowners", "What 'buildings insurance' actually covers" |
| Existing homeowners | "Are you one of the 70% of UK homeowners who are underinsured?", "The £10K surprise: hidden costs of buying a home", "Insurance renewal: why you should never auto-renew" |
| Landlords | "Landlord insurance vs home insurance: what you actually need", "Liability risks every landlord should know about" |
| Investors | "The £60B data problem in UK home insurance", "Why insurers are paying £1.22 for every £1 in premiums" |

**Formats:** Data-driven articles, calculators ("Am I underinsured?"), comparison tools, market analysis reports
**Primary channel:** Blog/SEO, PR, LinkedIn, email
**KPI:** Calculator usage, lead generation, PR pickup, partner interest

### Pillar 3: Build Real Value
**Editorial mission:** Show homeowners how to increase their property's value through smart maintenance, improvements, and green upgrades — and show investors the net-zero data opportunity.

**Why it matters:** This pillar ties into the net-zero regulatory tailwind. The UK government's 2050 net-zero target, MEES regulations for landlords, and green mortgage products all create demand for verified property energy data — exactly what HomeTruth provides.

| Audience | Content examples |
|---|---|
| First-time buyers | "Which home improvements actually add value?", "Your first year of homeownership: a maintenance calendar" |
| Existing homeowners | "The EPC upgrade guide: what to do and what it costs", "Home maintenance that pays for itself", "Smart home tech that actually saves money" |
| Landlords | "MEES 2028: what landlords need to do now", "Green retrofits that increase rental yield", "The compliance cost of ignoring EPC ratings" |
| Investors | "Green homes built on truth: the data layer for net-zero", "Why verified energy data is the next infrastructure play" |

**Formats:** Guides, seasonal maintenance calendars, ROI calculators, case studies, net-zero research
**Primary channel:** Blog/SEO, Pinterest, YouTube, email newsletter
**KPI:** Return visits, document vault signups, content shares

### Pillar 4: The Property Intelligence Revolution
**Editorial mission:** Position HomeTruth as a thought leader building the trust layer for UK property. Aimed primarily at investors, partners, and industry — but with occasional consumer-facing pieces that make people go "I had no idea it was that broken."

**Why it matters:** This is the investor-facing pillar. It demonstrates that HomeTruth understands the market deeply, has a defensible thesis, and is building something structurally important — not just another app.

| Audience | Content examples |
|---|---|
| Industry/investors | "The £9 trillion trust deficit", "Why every property will need a digital logbook by 2030", "From paper to platform: the case for a UK property data layer" |
| Consumer crossover | "Why your home doesn't have a service history (and why it should)", "The car vs house comparison: how property data fell behind" |
| Partners | "What verified property data means for underwriting", "The insurtech opportunity in property intelligence" |

**Formats:** Long-form essays, research reports, founder posts (LinkedIn), conference talks, press commentary, data visualisations
**Primary channel:** LinkedIn, industry press, investor updates, HomeTruth blog
**KPI:** LinkedIn engagement, press coverage, investor pipeline, partnership enquiries

## 9. What the Website Needs to Change

The current hometruth.io communicates the vision and hosts the AI Advisor well. To support a full content strategy, it needs:

1. **A consumer entry point** — a clear homeowner-first landing experience alongside the existing platform narrative. "Every home has a truth. Know yours." with a prominent CTA to try the AI Advisor.

2. **A blog/content hub** — this is where all pillar content lives. Must be SEO-optimised, well-categorised, and integrated with the AI advisor.

3. **Dedicated investor section** — a clear "For Investors" entry point that surfaces the existing platform vision, market data, and traction content.

4. **Interactive tools** — the "Home Check" quiz, the insurance calculator, the maintenance calendar. These drive engagement and data.

5. **Social proof** — user stories, testimonials, the STYX endorsement prominently displayed, press logos.

## 10. The One-Page Summary

**Creative platform:** "Every home has a truth. Know yours."

**Four pillars:**

| Pillar | Mission | Primary audience |
|---|---|---|
| Know Your Home | Educate homeowners about their property | First-time buyers, homeowners |
| Protect Your Investment | Demystify insurance and financial risk | Homeowners, landlords |
| Build Real Value | Smart maintenance, improvements, green upgrades | All consumer segments |
| The Property Intelligence Revolution | Thought leadership for industry and investors | Investors, partners |

**Three things that make this strategy work:**

1. **The AI advisor IS the content product.** Every article, guide, and tool should funnel people toward trying the advisor. Content and product are the same thing.

2. **Dual-purpose content.** The best pieces serve consumers AND investors simultaneously. "70% of UK homes are underinsured" is a helpful article for homeowners and a market-sizing proof point for investors.

3. **Monty is the secret weapon.** A co-founder with deep media relationships, a journalist's instinct for story, and a public profile is an unfair advantage. The content strategy should put him front and centre as the voice of HomeTruth.
