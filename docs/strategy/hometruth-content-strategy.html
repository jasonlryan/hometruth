Section 01

The Strategic Problem This Solves

HomeTruth has two audiences with fundamentally different needs — investors and homeowners — and the content strategy needs to serve both simultaneously.

The public website at hometruth.io does a strong job of communicating the platform vision, the dual-flywheel model, and the market opportunity. It also hosts the live AI Advisor. But there is currently no ongoing content layer — no blog, no guides, no editorial, no SEO-driven articles — that gives homeowners a reason to discover HomeTruth organically, return regularly, and build trust before they engage with the product.

Meanwhile, the competitive landscape is moving fast. The UK government is actively exploring mandating digital property logbooks. Home Owner's Passport already has Land Registry integration and is piloting with estate agents. Hometree has 100,000+ customers for home services subscriptions. The window to establish HomeTruth as the definitive voice on property intelligence is open, but it won't stay open.

This content strategy adds the missing layer: it gives homeowners a reason to engage (feeding the consumer flywheel), it gives investors evidence of traction and market positioning, and it builds an organic acquisition engine that reduces CAC over time.

Section 02

Audience Analysis

Primary B2C

First-Time Buyers

Who: 25-38, likely renting, saving for a deposit, anxious and overwhelmed by the biggest financial commitment of their lives.

What they feel: Fear of making a mistake. Information overload. Distrust of estate agents. Excitement mixed with dread.

Need: Guidance they can trust from someone not trying to sell them something. Plain-English explainers. Tools that make them feel in control.

Primary B2C

Existing Homeowners

Who: 30-65, already own a home, juggling maintenance, insurance, and improvements with busy lives.

What they feel: Vaguely anxious they're not looking after their biggest asset properly. Frustrated by insurance opacity. Curious what their home is worth now.

Need: Practical help. Reminders and schedules. Clarity on insurance. Confidence their home is maintaining value.

Secondary B2C

Landlords & Investors

Who: 35-65, own 1-10+ properties, focused on yield and compliance. From accidental landlords to professional portfolio managers.

What they feel: Stressed by ever-changing regulations. Overwhelmed by compliance across multiple properties.

Need: Compliance tracking. Multi-property document management. Clarity on MEES and EPC obligations.

B2B

Investors & Partners

Who: Seed-stage investors, insurtechs, insurance executives, mortgage brokers, green finance providers.

What they feel: Looking for data-backed opportunities. Sceptical of another PropTech pitch. Interested in the verified data angle.

Need: Evidence of traction. Market analysis. Clear business model. Proof the team can execute.

Section 03

The Creative Platform

The Underlying Human Truth

"My home is the biggest investment of my life, but I know more about my car than I do about my house."

This is the tension HomeTruth exists to resolve. Cars have service histories, MOT records, and logbooks. Homes — worth ten times more — have a drawer full of paper and a vague memory of when the boiler was last serviced. The information asymmetry is absurd, and everyone feels it even if they can't articulate it.

"Every home has a truth. Know yours."

The idea that unites everything — across all audiences, all channels, all content.

The word "truth" is the brand's superpower. It implies transparency, trust, data integrity, and honesty — all the things the property market currently lacks. Every piece of content should make the reader feel like they're getting closer to the truth about their home.

For Homeowners

Your home has a story — its real condition, its true value, its hidden risks, its untapped potential. HomeTruth helps you see it clearly.

For Investors

The UK property market runs on bad data. HomeTruth is building the infrastructure to fix that. The truth is the product.

For the Industry

Insurers, lenders, and brokers are making billion-pound decisions on incomplete information. HomeTruth gives them the truth they need.

Section 04

Content Pillars

Four pillars. Each serves multiple audiences. Each has a clear editorial mission.

Pillar 1: Know Your Home

Help homeowners understand what they own — its value, its risks, its documents, its true condition.

This is the acquisition engine. Every homeowner who searches "what EPC rating do I need" or "how much is my house worth" is a potential user. This pillar turns HomeTruth into a trusted resource before anyone downloads the app.

First-time buyers

Example Content

"The first-time buyer's document checklist" · "What your survey actually tells you" · "EPCs explained in 5 minutes"

Homeowners

Example Content

"10 things you should know about your home but probably don't" · "What's in your deeds" · "How to read your home insurance policy"

Landlords

Example Content

"The landlord's compliance Bible" · "Document management for multi-property portfolios"

Formats: Long-form guides, interactive checklists, short explainer videos, infographics, AI advisor demos
Primary channel: Blog/SEO, YouTube, social media
KPI: Organic traffic, time on page, email signups

Pillar 2: Protect Your Investment

Demystify home insurance, reduce financial risk, and help homeowners make smarter money decisions about their property.

Insurance is HomeTruth's first B2B application. Content that exposes the data gap in insurance builds both consumer awareness and investor confidence. The stat that 70% of UK properties are underinsured is a content goldmine.

First-time buyers

Example Content

"Home insurance: the complete guide for new homeowners" · "What 'buildings insurance' actually covers"

Homeowners

Example Content

"Are you one of the 70% who are underinsured?" · "The £10K surprise: hidden costs of buying a home" · "Insurance renewal: why you should never auto-renew"

Investors

Example Content

"The £60B data problem in UK home insurance" · "Why insurers are paying £1.22 for every £1 in premiums"

Formats: Data-driven articles, calculators ("Am I underinsured?"), comparison tools, market analysis reports
Primary channel: Blog/SEO, PR, LinkedIn, email
KPI: Calculator usage, lead generation, PR pickup, partner interest

Pillar 3: Build Real Value

Show homeowners how to increase their property's value through smart maintenance, improvements, and green upgrades — and show investors the net-zero data opportunity.

This pillar ties into the net-zero regulatory tailwind. The UK government's 2050 net-zero target, MEES regulations for landlords, and green mortgage products all create demand for verified property energy data — exactly what HomeTruth provides.

First-time buyers

Example Content

"Which home improvements actually add value?" · "Your first year of homeownership: a maintenance calendar"

Landlords

Example Content

"MEES 2028: what landlords need to do now" · "Green retrofits that increase rental yield" · "The compliance cost of ignoring EPC ratings"

Investors

Example Content

"Green homes built on truth: the data layer for net-zero" · "Why verified energy data is the next infrastructure play"

Formats: Guides, seasonal maintenance calendars, ROI calculators, case studies, net-zero research
Primary channel: Blog/SEO, Pinterest, YouTube, email newsletter
KPI: Return visits, document vault signups, content shares

Pillar 4: The Property Intelligence Revolution

Position HomeTruth as a thought leader building the trust layer for UK property. Aimed primarily at investors, partners, and industry.

This is the investor-facing pillar. It demonstrates that HomeTruth understands the market deeply, has a defensible thesis, and is building something structurally important — not just another app.

Industry / Investors

Consumer Crossover

Partners

Formats: Long-form essays, research reports, founder posts (LinkedIn), conference talks, press commentary, data visualisations
Primary channel: LinkedIn, industry press, investor updates, HomeTruth blog
KPI: LinkedIn engagement, press coverage, investor pipeline, partnership enquiries

Content Priorities: First 90 Days

Month 1

Foundation

  • Launch blog with 8-10 foundational SEO articles across all four pillars
  • Publish "The Property Trust Gap" — flagship data report for investors and press
  • Set up email capture and weekly newsletter
  • Begin Monty's LinkedIn cadence (3x/week)
  • Create the "HomeTruth Home Check" — free interactive tool

Month 2

Engine

  • Publish 8-10 more SEO articles on highest-volume keywords
  • Launch "The Truth About..." content series
  • First YouTube video: "Why your home doesn't have a service history"
  • Instagram/TikTok launch: "Home Truth or Myth?" series
  • Pitch first opinion piece to national press

Month 3

Acceleration

  • Publish Brighton-specific content (local SEO for pilot market)
  • First case study: early AI advisor user stories
  • Launch the insurance calculator ("Are you underinsured?")
  • Second data report: "The Net Zero Data Gap"

Section 09

What the Website Needs to Change

The current hometruth.io communicates the vision and hosts the AI Advisor well. To support a full content strategy, it needs five additions:

1. Consumer Entry Point

A clear homeowner-first landing experience alongside the existing platform narrative. "Every home has a truth. Know yours." with a prominent CTA to try the AI Advisor.

2. Blog / Content Hub

Where all pillar content lives. SEO-optimised, well-categorised, and integrated with the AI advisor.

3. Investor Section

The current site content, moved behind a clear "For Investors" link. Don't lose it; reframe it.

4. Interactive Tools

The "Home Check" quiz, the insurance calculator, the maintenance calendar. These drive engagement and data.

5. Social Proof

User stories, testimonials, the STYX endorsement prominently displayed, press logos.
