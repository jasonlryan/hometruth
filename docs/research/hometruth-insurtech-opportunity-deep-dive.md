A stark case study from the Financial Ombudsman Service highlights a situation where a homeowner, having estimated their property's rebuild cost at £400,000, was found to have a true rebuild cost of £800,000 following a claim. The insurer, applying the \"average clause,\" consequently reduced the claim payout by 50%, leaving the policyholder with a massive financial shortfall.28 Such outcomes are not only detrimental to the customer but also create significant reputational and regulatory risk for the insurer, particularly under the stringent requirements of the Financial Conduct Authority's (FCA) Consumer Duty, which mandates that firms deliver \"good outcomes\" for retail customers.29 By providing a verified ledger of essential property characteristics—such as year built, square footage, construction materials, and an accurate rebuild cost derived from modern AVMs—HomeTruth directly addresses the root cause of underinsurance.6  \nThe industry is acutely aware of this problem and is investing heavily in AI-powered underwriting platforms to automate and enhance risk assessment.9 However, the efficacy of these sophisticated AI models is entirely contingent on the quality and integrity of the data they are fed.
